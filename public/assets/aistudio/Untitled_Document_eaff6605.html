First-Year Roadmap: Java + DSA Foundation (Oct 2026 – Apr 2027)

Drafted Oct 19, 2026 · Window: 6 months, Oct 19, 2026 to about Apr 19, 2027

The goal in one paragraph

By Apr 2027: solid Java, about 150 DSA problems he can re-solve, 2 projects on GitHub, and clear spoken explanations. Google is the long-term target; this is the foundation phase.

Assumptions (please correct these)

  • About 2 hours on most weekdays (sometimes more) and 5 to 6 hours on each weekend day, so roughly 15 to 18 hours a week.
  • He is in sem 1 and has done no DSA yet. His Java level is “some”, so Week 1 includes a quick check (below) to decide whether Month 1 can be shortened.
  • Exam dates are unknown. In exam weeks he drops to “light mode”: 30 to 45 minutes a day of revising old problems, nothing new.

Weekly routine

Month 1 (Oct 19 to Nov 19): Java basics

Month 2 (Nov 19 to Dec 19): Collections, arrays, hashing

Month 3 (Dec 19 to Jan 19): Two pointers, sliding window, binary search

Month 4 (Jan 19 to Feb 19): Stack, queue, linked list

Month 5 (Feb 19 to Mar 19): Trees and recursion

Month 6 (Mar 19 to Apr 19): Consolidate
